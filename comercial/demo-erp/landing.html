QUESTIONS

Before you switch it on.

Can modules be added or removed later?

Yes. Every module shares the same database as the rest, so adding one later means no migration. Inside the company, each tool can also be granted or removed per person.

How do we load the data we already have in Excel?

Inventory is imported from CSV on its own screen. Clients, open contracts and the rest are reviewed case by case during setup.

Who sees what inside the company?

Management sees everything. A sales agent sees their own leads, their own deals and the projects assigned to them. The database enforces that separation, not just the screen.

Where is my data?

In a PostgreSQL database of your own, managed on Supabase, with row-level security and scheduled backups. This demo does not touch any real database.
